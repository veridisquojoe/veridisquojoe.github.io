From my time in the UVA School of Data Science. A team and I used a variety of techniques to predict movie scores. This is some of the work from that project, where we used k-means clustering to explore whether there were organic clusters between models when certain features were and weren't included. It was a way to use supervised learning for data exploration before moving on to learning with unlabeled data.
